Help test Karinderya Life

Run Lola's karinderya: cook Filipino ulam, serve your neighbors and turn them into suki. The game is free. Play it before anyone else. All you need is an Android phone and a Gmail account.

Opened this from Facebook or Messenger? Tap ⋮ (top right) → Open in Chrome or Open in browser first. The steps below only work in Chrome, signed in to your Gmail.

1Join the tester group

Use the same Gmail that's signed in to Google Play on your phone. Tap Join group on the page that opens.

Join the group

2Become a tester

Wait a minute after joining, then tap Become a tester.

Become a tester

3Download the game

Install it from Google Play like any app. Updates come automatically.

Download on Google Play
Please stay in the test for at least 14 days and keep the game installed. Google needs this before the game can come out for everyone. Play when you like. Salamat po! 🍲

Something not working?

If Play says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after step 2 and try again, and check that you're signed in with the same Gmail. Feedback or problems: karinderyalife@gmail.com.
