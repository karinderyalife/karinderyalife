Karinderya Life

Privacy policy

Updated 27 November 2026

Karinderya Life is a cooking and serving game: you run a small Filipino eatery, cook the ulam and serve your neighbors. This page says what the game does with your data.

What we keep

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not connect to the internet. Your progress (days played, money, dishes, items and settings) is saved only on your phone.

We never collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any device id. The game asks for no phone permissions.

Who sees it

No one. Since nothing leaves your phone, nothing is shared or sold.

How long

Your saved game stays on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings. Either one deletes it completely.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloading and updating the game. Google's own privacy policy covers that (policies.google.com/privacy).

Changes

If a future version adds ads, in-app purchases or donations, this page will be updated first to say exactly what those collect and who handles them.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you can ask what we keep. Since we keep nothing, there is nothing to give or delete, but you can always ask. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
